# Leo filming and measurement protocol (normal reference, 7 kg mixed breed)

Goal: a Grade 0 reference for the same joint-angle metrics used on the simulation (stifle, hock, hip ROM per stride, stride frequency, speed).

## Measurements (once, with a soft tape; a photo of each is fine)
Standing, side view, relaxed. Record in cm (and Leo's weight in kg):
1. Shoulder height (floor to the top of the shoulder blade / withers)
2. Hip height (floor to the greater trochanter, the bony point at the hip)
3. Femur length (greater trochanter to the stifle/knee)
4. Tibia length (stifle to the hock point)
5. Metatarsus length (hock to the paw joint)
6. Body length (point of shoulder to point of buttock)

## Video
- Phone on a tripod or a stable surface, **side view, camera axis perpendicular to the walking line**, lens at about hip height, 2-4 m away so the whole dog and 3-4 strides stay in frame.
- 60 fps or higher (120/240 fps slow-motion if available), 1080p, fixed zoom, no panning (keep the camera still, let the dog cross the frame), good daylight.
- Flat surface, dog on a loose leash, a steady trot (not a walk, not a gallop). Aim for 10 passes in each direction (both sides are seen).
- Optional but very helpful: small tape/sticker markers at the hip (greater trochanter), stifle, hock, paw joint and shoulder. Plain pale tape on dark fur or dark on pale fur.
- Put a ruler or a 1 m stick in the frame once for scale.
- Save the originals untouched; note the date, surface and approximate speed (measure 5 m with a stopwatch if possible).
